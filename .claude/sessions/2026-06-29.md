## Decisions

- **Deleted no page files** — used `tsconfig.app.json` exclude instead. Safer; preserves history; can re-enable pages later.
- **Canvas auto-load not touched** — `brainvis-canvas` already calls `loadData()` in its `ngAfterViewInit`. Menu-bar's dataset picker syncs on user interaction. No double-load.
- **Save button calls Study endpoints** — `saveGraphStudy/saveStoryStudy/saveTextReportStudy` are the study-specific save paths. IDcreator is cast as `any` since service signatures still type it as `number` but model accepts String.
